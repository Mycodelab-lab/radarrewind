The idea in one line

Radar is like a flashlight that bounces off the ground: water reflects it away (looks dark), rough things like crops and buildings bounce it back (look bright). Compare two dates and you see what moved.

Why NISAR

NISAR's L-band radar sees through clouds and works at night, and it revisits the same place about every 12 days. Its longer wavelength reaches down through crops and tree cover to the ground and water beneath.

How a change is classified

New open water: was above the water level, now below it
Water gone: water receded or field drained
Darker by more than the threshold: flooding, ploughed or bare soil, cleared or burned land
Brighter by more than the threshold: crops growing, new buildings, wet soil, flooded vegetation
